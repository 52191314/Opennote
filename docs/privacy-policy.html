1. Overview & Commitment

Welcome to Opennote (referred to as "the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), an open-source engineering diagram and handwriting notebook application distributed for Android under package name com.woaiyj1314.opennote and other cross-platform targets.

Opennote is developed with a strict privacy-first, local-first philosophy. We believe that your handwriting, thoughts, diagrams, and personal documents belong exclusively to you. The application operates completely standalone without requiring an account, registration, or internet connection.

Key Guarantee: We do not collect, monetize, sell, or rent your personal data, notes, or usage patterns to any third parties or advertising networks.

2. Data We Process & Collect

The application accesses and processes only the minimum data required to deliver core digital notebook functionality:

Data Category Storage Location Purpose & Handling Shared Externally?
Notes, Handwriting & Strokes Internal Device Storage Vector ink strokes, highlighters, mathematical formulas, and diagrams rendered in the editor. No (Never transmitted unless you opt to sync to your private cloud)
Imported Images & PDFs Internal App Directory Documents or images you deliberately import into a note canvas for annotation. No
In-App Preferences Device SharedPreferences Theme choices, pen color presets, tool widths, grid styles, and canvas display settings. No
Cloud Credentials Android Secure Keystore Server URI, username, and app password for Nextcloud / WebDAV sync. Encrypted with hardware-backed key. No (Only communicated directly to your chosen server)

3. Device Permissions & Scopes

To perform its duties on Android devices, Opennote requests the following explicit runtime permissions as defined in its AndroidManifest.xml:

Permission Type Necessity & Justification
android.permission.INTERNET Network Access Required only when the user voluntarily enables remote synchronization to their own Nextcloud server, or for opt-in error diagnostics. The app functions completely offline if internet is disabled.
android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE		 Storage / Files Used strictly for user-initiated file import and export operations (e.g. saving exported PDF notes to your Downloads directory or opening an existing .sbn or PDF file). We never scan unselected folders.
Scoped Storage Compliance: On modern Android versions (Android 11+ / API 30+), Opennote utilizes the Android Storage Access Framework (SAF) and scoped storage. We do not hold broad media access permissions (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, and READ_MEDIA_AUDIO are explicitly removed).

4. Optional Cloud Synchronization

Opennote offers optional cloud synchronization for users who desire seamless cross-device syncing across Android, Windows, macOS, Linux, and iOS.

Client-Side End-to-End Encryption

We employ a zero-knowledge encryption architecture:

  • All notes, drawings, and embedded media are encrypted locally on your device before they leave your device using strong cryptographic standards (AES).
  • A unique cryptographic encryption key is generated on your device and stored in the Android Secure Keystore via flutter_secure_storage.
  • Neither the server administrator, cloud hosting provider, nor the Opennote development team has access to your decryption key or unencrypted content.

Self-Hosted & Third-Party Nextcloud Servers

You are free to sync with your own self-hosted Nextcloud instance or any trusted third-party WebDAV provider. Any data transmitted to your server is governed by your server's hosting terms and security configuration.

5. Diagnostics & Crash Reporting

To diagnose fatal unexpected application crashes and maintain high stability on diverse hardware, Opennote includes integration with the open-source Sentry SDK.

Strict Opt-In Policy: In Opennote, telemetry is disabled by default. No crash logs or diagnostic traces are collected unless you explicitly provide consent in the application settings.

When explicitly enabled by the user, diagnostic reports may include:

  • Operating system version and device architecture (e.g., Android 14, ARM64).
  • Application version and build number.
  • Exception stack trace and error message leading to the crash.
  • A non-persistent, pseudorandom session identifier that resets upon application reinstallation.

We never collect: Personal identifiers, names, emails, handwriting data, canvas images, note titles, passwords, encryption keys, or persistent hardware identifiers (IMEI, MAC, or Google Advertising ID).

6. Third-Party Services

Opennote minimizes third-party service dependencies:

  • Nextcloud / WebDAV (Optional): Only active when configured by the user. User-selected cloud provider.
  • Sentry (Optional & Opt-In): Crash analysis service. Complies with GDPR and SOC-2 data security standards. Sentry Privacy Policy.
  • No Ad Networks: We do not integrate Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, or any commercial advertising networks.

7. Data Retention & Deletion (Google Play Mandate)

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy regarding user data transparency, here is how you can permanently delete your data:

1. Deleting Local Application Data

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete individual notes, notebooks, or folders within the app. Deleted notes are permanently purged from your device filesystem.
  • Complete Wipe: To remove all local data, notes, and preferences at once:
    1. Open your device Settings > Apps > Opennote.
    2. Select Storage & cache > tap Clear storage (or Clear data).
    3. Alternatively, uninstalling Opennote permanently erases its sandboxed storage directory.

2. Deleting Cloud Data

  • If you have configured Nextcloud synchronization, deleting a note within the app will delete the corresponding encrypted remote file during the next sync cycle.
  • To permanently delete all remote data, log in to your Nextcloud/WebDAV web portal and delete the Opennote / Saber sync directory, or delete your user account directly on your server.

3. Data Deletion Support

If you have questions regarding data deletion or need verification, you may submit a request at any time to xianspired@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Opennote is a general audience productivity and note-taking tool. It is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions governed by GDPR-K).

Because Opennote does not require an account and stores data locally, we do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children. If you believe a minor has inadvertently submitted personal information to our email or support channels, please contact us immediately, and we will delete the data without delay.

9. Security Safeguards

We employ robust technical and organizational security controls to protect your notes:

  • Operating System Sandboxing: Notes are confined within Android's sandboxed user storage, preventing other installed apps from reading your files.
  • Hardware-Backed Keystore: All sensitive cloud credentials and encryption keys are safeguarded in the device's secure enclave / Android Keystore.
  • Transport Layer Security: All network communications with Nextcloud servers or diagnostic endpoints use modern HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption in transit.
  • Open Source Auditability: Our source code is fully accessible under the GNU General Public License v3.0, allowing continuous security audits by the open-source community.

10. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA Disclosures)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and applicable international laws, you possess fundamental rights:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You can export your notes to standard, non-proprietary formats (PDF, PNG, SVG, or JSON-based .sbn) at any time with zero vendor lock-in.
  • Right to Rectification & Deletion: You have absolute power to modify, rewrite, or delete any note or setting directly on your device.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Diagnostic reporting consent can be enabled or revoked instantaneously via the application settings without affecting note-taking functionality.
  • Non-Discrimination: We provide identical note-taking features regardless of your privacy choices.
