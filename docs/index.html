Privacy by architecture, not policy fine print.

Opennote (com.woaiyj1314.opennote) is open source under GPLv3. Your handwriting stays on your device by default.

Local-First

No Mandatory Account

Notes, PDFs, and drawings are stored locally in sandboxed device storage. No sign-up required.

Zero-Knowledge

Client-Side AES Encryption

Optional Nextcloud/WebDAV sync encrypts every file on-device before upload. Decryption keys stay in your device keystore.

Zero Trackers

No Ads or Data Brokers

Zero advertising SDKs and zero behavioral analytics. Crash reporting is strictly opt-in and disabled by default.
